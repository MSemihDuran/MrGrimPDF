Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MrGrimPDF

Is MrGrimPDF really 100% free with no hidden charges?

Yes, MrGrimPDF is completely free, open-source, and unrestricted. There are no paid tiers, watermarks on your output files, or credit card requirements.

Are my files and documents secure?

Absolutely. When run locally on your computer, your documents never leave your machine. In the cloud, files are processed in isolated sessions and automatically purged after processing.

Can I convert scanned PDF to Word or Excel?

Yes! MrGrimPDF includes OCR (Optical Character Recognition) supporting Turkish, English, German, French, and Spanish to turn scanned documents into editable text, Word documents, or Excel tables.

Can I use MrGrimPDF on mobile devices?

Yes, MrGrimPDF is designed from the ground up to be 100% responsive on smartphones and tablets. You can draw signatures, merge, split, and convert documents directly in mobile browsers.
